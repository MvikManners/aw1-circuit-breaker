Wallet Status: 🟢 GREEN

Phase 3: The Skin

Goal: Asset Appreciation. Transform your vehicle from a machine into a lucrative financial asset. Covers final aesthetic restorations, interior refinement, and tuning to return the car to its original "showroom soul". The Wealth Dividend: Combined with the immutable record in your Digital Safety Passport, Phase 3 ensures your vehicle commands a guaranteed 15% resale premium over unverified cars.

🔄 Routine Maintenance: The Fluid Gospel

To protect your investment, everyday maintenance is strictly governed by the Maintenance Covenant:

  • The Synthetic Bloodline: Engine oil and filters are replaced exactly every 7,500km, utilizing exclusively synthetic grade oil and OEM filters.
  • The Cooling Audit: Annual pressure test of your radiator, flushing out corrosive tap water and replacing it with specialized coolant to prevent internal oxidation.
  • The 6-Month Re-Torque: Mandatory bi-annual check to re-torque the "Big 10" suspension bolts to OEM spec as parts settle.
