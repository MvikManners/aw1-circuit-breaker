## 1. PRE-RECOVERY: ENVIRONMENT PREPARATION
1. Ensure the new environment has `python3.10`, `sqlite3`, and the `cryptography` library installed.
2. Ensure you have the `BACKUP_ENCRYPTION_KEY` available (Found in your .env or secure vault).
3. Identify the most recent '.zip.enc' file from your off-site storage.

## 2. DECRYPTION & EXTRACTION
Execute the following process to unlock the system payload:
1. Run a script containing:
    from cryptography.fernet import Fernet
    cipher_suite = Fernet(SECRET_KEY)
    with open('ledger_YYYY-MM-DD_HHMMSS.zip.enc', 'rb') as f:
        data = cipher_suite.decrypt(f.read())
    with open('system_restored.zip', 'wb') as f:
        f.write(data)
2. Unzip `system_restored.zip` to a temporary directory.

## 3. STRUCTURAL RESTORATION
1. Move `gospel_v4.db` to `/home/LavetoLab/instance/`.
2. Move `/static/` assets (images, CSS, manifests) back to their respective folders.
3. Verify integrity:
    sqlite3 /home/LavetoLab/instance/gospel_v4.db "PRAGMA integrity_check;"
    (Expected output: 'ok')

## 4. RE-INITIATION
1. Restart the application server.
2. Initialize the Sentinel daemon:
    python3.10 /home/LavetoLab/vault_sync.py &
3. Verify logs in `/home/LavetoLab/logs/vault_sync.log`.
