Part I: Frequently Asked Questions

Why is Laveto different from a normal garage?

Traditional garages operate on hidden costs and unverified parts. Laveto is a membership-based system that combines a dedicated savings account with strict, photo-verified vehicle maintenance to completely eliminate surprise repair bills.

How does the Price Guarantee work?

We bypass local retail shops and buy directly from international suppliers. We guarantee that the parts we install will cost you exactly 80% of the Gaborone retail average, saving you 20% immediately.

How does the Savings Account earn interest?

Your monthly deposit is split: 40% goes into your Maintenance Fund (available instantly for repairs), and 60% goes into your Savings Balance. This balance is used to fund internal loans, generating interest that is paid back into your account.

What is the Digital Service Record?

It is a permanent, secure database storing every photo, invoice, and inspection report for your car. Having this verified history increases your vehicle's resale value by up to 15%.
