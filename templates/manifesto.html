Laveto System Restoration rejects this premise. Through disciplined engineering and transparent service history, we transform the vehicle into a Managed Asset: a reliable machine that holds its value through undeniable proof of care.

THE 15% RESALE PREMIUM

Vehicles maintained within the Laveto System do not compete with the standard used car market. They exist in an elite tier of verified reliability. This documented history commands a proven 15% valuation premium when it is time to sell.

II. Verified Restoration

We do not perform "quick fixes." Quick fixes are temporary patches applied to symptoms. We perform Verified Restoration.

Restoration is the process of rescuing a vehicle from unregulated garages and returning it to factory standards. Every part, every fluid, and every inspection photo is permanently logged in the Digital Service Record.

III. The Maintenance Fund

A vehicle without a repair budget is a liability. A vehicle backed by a Maintenance Fund is secure.

By attaching a dedicated savings account directly to your vehicle's VIN, we ensure its future is already paid for. You no longer need to fear unexpected breakdowns; the vehicle is self-sustaining, actively earning interest, and always ready for the road.
